Trends to Watch

1. The end of DTC as we know it

With FDA enforcement surging and pharma ad dollars shifting away from linear TV, budgets are flowing into digital, streaming and direct-to-patient commerce platforms. Brands should plan for a compliance-first, digital-first channel mix.

2. Agentic AI — with a disclosure catch

AI is moving from insight to execution in pharma marketing, but regulators and audiences increasingly expect clear labelling of AI-generated and sponsored content.

3. EU pharma reform enters implementation mode

The new EU pharmaceutical legislation takes effect in 2026 with a transition period through 2028. Dutch and European marketers should track variation, transparency and availability rules now rather than later.
