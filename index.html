NL

CBG: verzoek tot overname handelsvergunning Emend

MSD will stop marketing Emend (aprepitant) in the EU and the Netherlands from 20 November for business reasons. CBG calls on other companies to take over the licence, as the 125 mg powder is the only aprepitant option for children aged 6 months to 12 years and is considered a critical medicine.

CHMP meeting highlights, 9-12 November 2026

EMA's CHMP recommended seven new medicines, including AstraZeneca's Baxfendy (baxdrostat) for hypertension, plus 11 extensions of indication. The committee also agreed a DHPC on Mounjaro and oral contraceptives, advising a barrier or non-oral method.

Trends to Watch

1. Supply continuity as brand trust

The Emend withdrawal and KNMP's call for structural fixes show Dutch stakeholders judge companies on availability, not only on innovation.

2. Regulatory capacity and timing uncertainty

FDA staffing gaps and the paused priority voucher pilot make launch dates less predictable, so flexible launch communication plans matter.

3. Patient-access data and partnerships

Hub analytics platforms and pharmacist-backed action plans point to access and collaboration as the new centre of commercial strategy.
