Trends to Watch

  1. Direct-to-patient goes mainstream. DTC/DTP discount platforms are shifting from experiment to expected infrastructure at nearly every major pharma company.
  2. Compliance is moving faster than campaigns. Accelerated FDA enforcement, the EU's digital-labeling shift, and KNMP's refreshed social media code all point the same direction: review cycles need to tighten.
  3. AI cuts both ways. The same AI reshaping HCP engagement and field sales is now generating consumer-facing "influencers" — expanding reach while raising new authenticity questions.
