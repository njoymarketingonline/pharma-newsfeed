CBG: antibioticaresistentie groeit, aandacht voor verstandig antibioticagebruik

During World AMR Awareness Week (18-24 November), the CBG is posting social media explainers on common facts and myths about antibiotics, citing WHO's estimate of five million deaths a year linked to resistance. It shows how Dutch regulators use simple, myth-busting content for public education.

The NZa postponed the new funding for smoking-cessation care from 1 January to 1 April 2027 after an objection by Zorgverzekeraars Nederland. Nicotine replacement products get a separate performance code, which matters for brands and pharmacy programmes in this category.

Gewijzigde doseeradviezen voor apixaban en edoxaban bij verminderde nierfunctie

From 1 December 2026 the G-Standaard will carry new dosing advice for apixaban and edoxaban in atrial fibrillation patients with severe kidney disease or on dialysis, aligned with the NFN guideline. Medical and HCP communication for anticoagulants should reflect the updated pharmacist guidance.

FDA requires NAION warning across the GLP-1 class

European Pharmaceutical Review's regulatory roundup reports that the FDA is requiring a warning for non-arteritic anterior ischaemic optic neuropathy (NAION) across all GLP-1 receptor agonists. Promotional materials and safety information for GLP-1 brands will need updating.

FDA OPDP issues untitled letter to Janssen Biotech for INLEXZO

The FDA's Office of Prescription Drug Promotion issued an untitled letter to Janssen Biotech on 17 November 2026 about promotion of INLEXZO (gemcitabine intravesical system). It follows recent letters to Vanda and Evolus, a sign that OPDP enforcement remains active.

Trends to Watch

1. Public education as a regulator tool

The CBG's AMR myth-busting campaign shows that short, plain-language social content is now standard for Dutch health authorities, and a good benchmark for compliant disease-awareness work.

2. Safety and promotion scrutiny

A class-wide GLP-1 label change and a new OPDP untitled letter are reminders to keep promotional materials and fair-balance information current.

3. Access shapes launch success

From precision oncology reimbursement in Europe to in-clinic psychedelic therapy in the US, commercial outcomes depend more and more on access pathways, not only on approval.
