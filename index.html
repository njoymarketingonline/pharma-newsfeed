FDA Launches Unprecedented Crackdown on Deceptive Drug Advertising
The FDA has intensified enforcement against misleading direct-to-consumer pharmaceutical advertisements, dispatching thousands of warning letters over six months—more than the entire preceding decade. The agency reports that 88% of advertisements for top-selling drugs violate FDA fair-balance guidelines.
Source: FDA News

Source: FDA News
