🔭 Trends to Watch

AI-Generated Content & Compliance: As agentic AI enters pharma marketing workflows, EU and US regulators are beginning to address disclosure requirements for AI-generated promotional content. Marketers should build governance frameworks now, before formal rules arrive.
Digital Patient Information in Europe: The pan-European shift to electronic Product Information (ePI) — backed by EMA, EFPIA and national agencies including the CBG — will reshape patient and HCP communications. Early movers in digital-first product information will gain a compliance and user experience advantage.
Supply Resilience as a Brand Issue: Medicine shortages and the new Critical Medicines Act are pushing supply chain resilience onto the marketing agenda. Companies communicating proactively about availability, access programmes and supply commitments will differentiate in a trust-sensitive market.
