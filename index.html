Trends to Watch

  1. Access becomes a marketing discipline. Pricing, transparency and direct-to-patient fulfillment are moving from operational afterthought to core brand strategy — the line between "commercial" and "marketing" is blurring.
  2. AI-driven, multichannel HCP engagement is going mainstream. Next-best-action models fed by sales and rep data are replacing static territory plans, though EU privacy rules keep adoption slower than in the US.
  3. Regulators on both sides of the Atlantic are tightening digital oversight. Dutch and EU authorities are cracking down on illegal online drug marketing and AI-generated health misinformation, while the FDA signals more scrutiny of DTC and social ads.
