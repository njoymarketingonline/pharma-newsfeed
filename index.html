CBG-MEB and CCMO host Science Day 2026 on N-of-1 individualised therapies

CBG launches public registration calls for critical medicines

From April 2026, the CBG is publishing open calls for marketing authorisation holders to register medicines where a long-term shortage is expected or supply has been discontinued — a new lever to address Dutch market gaps and an opportunity for MAH portfolio teams.

Source: cbg-meb.nl

Europe

EU

CHMP recommends eight new medicines, including oral Wegovy

At its 18-21 May meeting, the EMA's human medicines committee backed Jascayd (nerandomilast) for pulmonary fibrosis, Vijoice (alpelisib) for PROS disorders, and an oral tablet line extension for Wegovy (semaglutide) in weight management — opening a major new front in obesity brand marketing.

Source: ema.europa.eu

EU

PRAC May meeting closes with no new referrals

The Pharmacovigilance Risk Assessment Committee met 4-7 May and concluded its agenda without initiating or closing any safety referrals — a quiet month that gives pharmacovigilance and promotional review teams space to focus on signal management routines.

Source: ema.europa.eu

EU

Joint AESGP, EFPIA and Medicines for Europe report on electronic Product Information

A new cross-industry report maps ePI pilot projects across 31 countries, signalling that digital medicines information is moving from concept to operating standard — and reshaping how labelling, omnichannel and HCP-facing assets will be built.

Source: efpia.eu

EU

EU General Pharmaceutical Legislation revision advances

Negotiators have reached provisional agreement on the GPL revision, with regulatory protection and market exclusivity provisions under active debate. Final adoption is now expected in 2028 — but commercial teams should already be modelling the impact on launch sequencing and lifecycle strategy.

Source: pharmaceutical-technology.com

EU

European Conference on Rare Diseases set for Prague, 3-4 June

EFPIA-supported conference will focus on "Rare Diseases in a Changing & Competitive Europe" — a key forum for rare-disease brand teams refining patient-advocacy partnerships and policy-aligned messaging.

Source: efpia.eu

Global

GLOBAL

FDA launches crackdown on misleading DTC advertising

The agency has issued thousands of warning letters and about 100 cease-and-desist notices to companies running deceptive ads, and is opening rulemaking to close the 1997 "adequate provision" loophole used to soften safety messaging in broadcast and digital. Expect tighter medical-legal-regulatory review cycles globally.

Source: fda.gov

GLOBAL

Pharma TV ad spend dips to $149M in May, AbbVie leads

The top-10 pharma TV spenders accounted for $149 million in May, down from $183 million in April and well below last May's $177 million. AbbVie's Humira topped the chart at $32.5 million — confirmation that dollars are continuing to migrate to digital and connected-TV environments.

Source: fiercepharma.com

GLOBAL

Pre-launch awareness investments produce a durable competitive edge

New research finds that oncology brands with high physician awareness at launch retain that lead long after launch — strengthening the case for early scientific narrative-building and disease-state campaigns 12-18 months ahead of approval.

Source: fiercepharma.com
